About Us

We started in a school library in 2019 with a notebook and a pencil. Today we are over 40 friends who love quick games, clever strategy and good snacks. Everyone is welcome, from first-timers to grandmasters of the center square.

Pro tip: start in the middle or a corner, and always watch for a fork!
